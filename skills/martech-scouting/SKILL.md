# Martech scouting

New tools appear daily; most solve a problem you do not have, or one an existing tool already solves. This skill starts from the job, screens hard, and trials only what could replace real effort.

## When to use

- A recurring scan for tools relevant to current priorities.
- Someone proposes a tool and you need a decision.
- Replacing a tool that is failing or too expensive.
- Reviewing the whole stack for overlap.

## Inputs

- Profile: `measurement` (current tool categories), `channels.active`, `company.team_size`, `approvals.spend`.
- The current stack and what each tool is used for; the job you want done and what it costs today in time or money.
- A record of tools already rejected and why, to avoid re-evaluating them.

## Process

1. **Start from the job:** what task, how often, who does it, what it costs now, and what "better" means (time saved, quality, a capability you lack).
2. **Scan sources:** launch sites, software review platforms, practitioner communities, newsletters and trend tools. Note where each candidate came from.
3. **Screen each candidate:** solves the stated job; overlaps with a tool you already pay for; integrates with your CRM and analytics; pricing at your scale; data handling and security posture; vendor stability; whether its terms allow your intended use (for example automation on third-party platforms).
4. **Shortlist two or three at most.**
5. **Trial plan (about 14 days):** one real use case, success criteria decided before starting, who tests, what data goes in (no customer data until security is checked), and the decision date.
6. **Decide:** buy, keep testing with a reason, or drop. Record the reason, including for drops, so the tool is not evaluated again next month.

## Output

```markdown
## Job to be done (task, frequency, current cost, definition of better)
## Candidates
| Tool | Source | Solves the job? | Overlap with stack | Integrations | Price at our scale | Data and terms check | Verdict |
## Trial plan (use case, success criteria, tester role, data allowed, decision date)
## Decision and reason (after the trial)
```

## Gates

- Vendor claims are assertions until tested ([evidence-and-claims](../b2b-growth-kit/policies/evidence-and-claims.md)).
- Paid plans, data uploads and connecting tools to company accounts follow [approval-gates](../b2b-growth-kit/policies/approval-gates.md).
- Do not adopt tools whose core feature depends on breaking another platform's terms.
