# Ad creative

Creative should make the right buyer think "that is my problem" in the first second, then show something believable. This skill builds real alternatives, not adjective swaps, and plans tests that teach you something.

## When to use

- Concepts and copy for search, social or display ads.
- Several genuinely different versions of a post, email, headline or hero.
- Short motion ads or product demo clips from screen recordings.
- A creative test plan for a new channel or offer.

## Inputs

- Profile: `positioning` (proof points), `offer`, `claims`, `voice`, `channels.active`.
- The channel, format and aspect ratio; the audience and its awareness level.
- Real proof material: screenshots, screen recordings, data, quotes with permission.

## Process

1. **Build the angle bank from the offer.** For each rung of the offer ladder, write 4-6 angles: the problem as the buyer says it, the deliverable, the contrast with the current alternative, a time-bound or event-specific angle, and the objection answered. Note which channel and awareness level each fits.
2. **Make variants on different axes.** Problem-led, outcome-led, contrarian, data-led, story-led, customer scenario, objection-first, short direct, long explanatory. Three variants with the same structure and different adjectives do not count. Label each with its axis, best use and risk, then recommend one.
3. **Fit the channel.** Search: the buyer's words, narrow claims. LinkedIn: role- and company-aware, reads like a useful post. Reddit and communities: plain, problem-first, honest company voice. Email: subject, first line, reason to care, one ask. Landing hero: headline, subline, CTA, proof cue.
4. **Static visual brief.** One message per image; real product UI, a ranked list or a before/after beats stock photos and abstract AI art. At most one short line of text on the image.
5. **Motion and video.** Inspect the source footage first and exclude recorder start-up, permission dialogs, credentials, notifications and dead waits. Pick one visual mechanism that explains the product action (focus, zoom, highlight, sort, compare, before/after). Keep UI readable on a phone; never shrink a full desktop screen into illegibility or blur the action being shown. Timing follows the duration and reading speed: the problem should be clear early and proof should appear before the midpoint. The ad must work muted, with captions. Scene plan fields are in the output below.
6. **Test matrix and stop rules.** Change one dimension at a time (problem, deliverable, contrast, specificity). Before launch, write the stop rules: low CTR after meaningful impressions means revise the hook; cheap clicks but no landing page engagement means a creative and page mismatch; engaged visits but no form starts means an offer or page problem, not creative.

## Output

```markdown
## Angle bank (angle, promise, fits channel and awareness level)
## Variants
### Version A: {axis}  (copy, best for, risk)
### Version B / C ...
Recommendation and why
## Visual brief or scene plan
| Scene | Source asset and timestamp | Caption or narration | Movement | Duration | Acceptance check |
## Test matrix and stop rules
```

If a rendered video is requested, a plan is not completion: render with an available tool, inspect sampled frames and playback, and report resolution, duration, legibility and any unverified audio.
