# LinkedIn outbound

LinkedIn outreach is a conversation with a real person under their own name. It works when the first touches give something useful and the ask comes late and small.

## When to use

- Starting outreach to a qualified contact list on LinkedIn.
- Adding LinkedIn touches to a cold email program.
- Re-engaging accepted connections who never replied.
- For public commenting and relationship building without a sales sequence, use [social-engagement](../social-engagement/SKILL.md).

## Inputs

- Profile: `voice.personas` (sender), `positioning`, `offer`, `outreach` (LinkedIn caps, suppression).
- Contacts from [prospect-list-builder](../prospect-list-builder/SKILL.md) with research angles and an owner.
- The sender's recent posts and the prospect's recent activity, if available.

## Process

1. **Assign ownership first.** One prospect, one owner across channels and teammates; record it in the CRM or list. Two people messaging the same person is the fastest way to look automated.
2. **Warm up (optional, days -7 to 0):** follow the person and leave one or two genuine comments on their posts. Skip it if they do not post.
3. **Sequence** (adjust to response):
   | Step | Day | Action | Content |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | 1 | 0 | Connection request | No note, or a short note tied to something specific (their post, company news); no link, no pitch |
   | 2 | +1 after accept | Value message | One useful insight or resource for their situation; no link-first, no ask |
   | 3 | +5 | Soft ask | "Worth a quick look at {specific thing}?" |
   | 4 | +10 | Close the loop | "If the timing is off, I'll step back." |
   Stop on any reply; route positives to [revenue-handoff](../revenue-handoff/SKILL.md).
4. **Copy rules:** under about 100 words per message; no pasted templates that read like templates ("I noticed..." openers are worn out); no price in the first touches; one question at most.
5. **Pacing:** stay under the profile's daily caps, spread actions through the day, weekdays only. Prefer manual sending or tools that operate within LinkedIn's terms; avoid anything marketed as bypassing limits.
6. **Measure:** acceptance rate, reply rate, positive reply rate, meetings. If acceptance is low, fix targeting or the sender's profile before rewriting messages.

## Output

```markdown
Sender persona / list / owner rule / daily caps
## Messages per step (with character or word counts)
## Personalization notes per prospect (the specific hook used)
## Pacing plan
## Metrics to watch and the threshold that triggers a change
```

## Gates

- Platform terms, caps, suppression and honest identity follow [outreach-compliance](../b2b-growth-kit/policies/outreach-compliance.md); run [send-preflight](../send-preflight/SKILL.md) first.
- Connection requests and messages are sends; they need approval for the list and copy ([approval-gates](../b2b-growth-kit/policies/approval-gates.md)).
- No scraped private data, no automation that breaks platform terms, no invented familiarity.
