# Query generation

## 1. Extract product facts

From the profile or the product site, collect:

- Positioning: hero copy, tagline.
- ICP: role wording ("for sales teams"), customer stories.
- Competitors: look for `/vs-*`, `/alternatives`, `/compare` pages; named competitors there are the most reliable list.
- Pricing tier: free versus paid threshold tells you whether buyers self-serve or need sales.

If the site is thin or the fetch fails, ask for a one-line positioning, three competitor names and the target buyer role. Do not invent them.

## 2. Cover these question types

| Type | Pattern | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| How-to | "how to do {core job} without {common workaround}" | Buyer is still researching and may not know a tool exists |
| Comparison | "{approach A} vs {approach B}" | Buyer is comparing approaches |
| Alternative | "{Competitor} alternatives" | Buyer knows a competitor and wants options; highest intent |
| Evaluation | "is {activity} worth {cost} for a {team size} team" | Buyer is judging return on effort |
| Problem statement | Variants of the common complaint about the manual process | Maps directly to the pain the product solves |

Example for project-management software: "how to track deliverables without a shared tool" (how-to), "kanban vs gantt chart" (comparison), "{Competitor} alternatives" (alternative).

Exclude pure brand queries ("what is {your product}"). They only measure existing awareness and do not guide GEO strategy.

## 3. Selection criteria

Keep a question only if it meets at least two:

- A concrete business scenario, not a broad industry term.
- Pre-purchase language: researching or comparing options, not support questions from long-time users.
- Alternatives exist, so there is a position worth taking.
- It maps to a capability the product has today. Winning a question you cannot deliver on wastes the win.
- The result can become a publishable asset (page, FAQ, comparison table).

Drop adjacent topics that would pull the brand away from its category.

## Estimating demand without paid keyword tools

- Search `"{query}" site:reddit.com`; real discussions mean buyers really ask it.
- Ask an answer engine what else people ask when researching the category; add the follow-ups to the candidate pool.
- Search autocomplete and "People also ask" are free volume signals.

Output: 15-25 questions, each tagged with its type and the criteria it met.
