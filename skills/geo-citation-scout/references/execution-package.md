# Execution package

Steps 1-5 produce findings. This step turns them into three deliverables: website and FAQ content, a social topic calendar, and a backlink plan. Each item must trace to measured data and real product information. Generic advice such as "post more on social" is not acceptable.

## Precondition: real differentiation input

Before writing any recommendation, get (from the user or existing material):

- Recent customer words: complaints or requests heard in sales calls, demos or support.
- The specific mechanism that makes the product different: not "we are better", but what it does that others do not (refresh frequency, a specific automated step).

Without this, the package collapses into generic advice and the analysis was wasted.

## Deliverable 1: website and FAQ content

For each high-priority question (weak or no citation):

- New page or rewrite of an existing one.
- Target H1 using the buyer's wording.
- The angle the page must answer, grounded in the differentiation input.
- The proven winning format to follow (listicle, comparison table, how-to) from the strong-source winners.

For each question you already win: re-check frequency, the source to watch, and what to do if you are displaced.

## Deliverable 2: social topic calendar

Topics come from the measured gaps or real customer feedback, never from a generic industry list. Each topic has: platform, angle (which question or which feedback), source material (real data or real conversations), and cadence.

Drafting:

- Follow the platform style guide if the team has one.
- Without one, review drafts for generic AI phrasing before treating them as final.
- Adapting one insight for several platforms means rewriting it for each, not swapping a few words.

Identity rules:

- **Personal or brand accounts** (LinkedIn, X, company blog): you can explain the method, use branded images, and share the same chart across your own accounts. That is normal cross-posting.
- **Community spaces** (Reddit, forums): follow each community's rules on self-promotion and disclose your affiliation when you mention your own product or analysis. Contribute the useful insight in the community's format; do not post disguised marketing.

Formats beyond pages: a question about a multi-step process can be answered with a visual step-by-step diagram. A genuinely useful how-to visual gets cited and re-shared, which also earns links.

## Deliverable 3: backlink plan

Start from third-party sites already shown to be cited by AI engines in step 2 (directories, review sites, publications), not from a generic "buy links" list.

- **Quick actions:** self-serve directory and listing submissions.
- **Long-term:** real customer reviews, guest articles, press coverage. These need real material, such as customers willing to review.
- **Content-led:** useful visuals and data are natural link magnets; plan them together with deliverable 1.

If the plan is blocked by a structural gap (for example, no customers willing to leave reviews), state it as the blocker. Buying links does not fix it; see [link-acquisition](../../link-acquisition/SKILL.md).

## Output

Three sections, each item tagged with the question and judgment it came from.
