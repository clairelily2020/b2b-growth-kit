# Social engagement

Public comments and replies make your name familiar before any sales message arrives. The posture is helpful and curious; the moment it turns into a pitch, it stops working.

## When to use

- Warming up target accounts before or alongside [linkedin-outbound](../linkedin-outbound/SKILL.md).
- Building an audience for the founder or brand among practitioners.
- Responding to industry conversations while they are fresh.

## Inputs

- Profile: `voice.personas` (who is engaging), `icp`, `positioning`, `channels`, `outreach` caps.
- A target list: accounts and people from [prospect-list-builder](../prospect-list-builder/SKILL.md), plus industry voices and creators.
- Ownership: which teammate engages which target, so one person never gets contacted by two of you.

## Process

1. **Daily ranges, not quotas** (adapt to capacity): about 10-15 thoughtful comments or replies, a few quote posts with a real point of view, and a small number of direct messages only where there is already public back-and-forth.
2. **Comment with substance:** add a specific experience, a data point, a counter-example or a good question. "Great post" and emoji-only replies do not count.
3. **Timing:** early comments on fresh posts get more visibility; do not let that push you into generic replies.
4. **Direct messages** come after several public exchanges, open with the specific thing they said, offer something useful (a resource, an intro, an observation), contain no link-first pitch and no demo ask.
5. **Connection requests** reference a real interaction; send them after engagement, not before.
6. **Handoff:** when someone asks about the product, pricing or a problem you solve, hand them to the owner of outbound or to [revenue-handoff](../revenue-handoff/SKILL.md) with the conversation context.
7. **Log** who you engaged with, where, and any signals, so the next touch builds on it.

## Output

```markdown
## Today's targets (person or account, recent post, planned action)
## Drafted comments and replies (each tied to the specific post)
## DMs drafted (only where public interaction exists)
## Signals worth handing off
```

## Gates

- Every comment and DM is individually written and reviewed by the person whose account it is. No copy-paste templates, no automation that breaks platform terms, no engagement pods.
- Direct messages and connection requests are sends: [outreach-compliance](../b2b-growth-kit/policies/outreach-compliance.md) and [approval-gates](../b2b-growth-kit/policies/approval-gates.md) apply.
- No selling in comments; disclose your affiliation when you mention your own product.
