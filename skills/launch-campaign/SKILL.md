# Launch campaign

A launch concentrates attention for a day or two. It pays off only if the product is understandable in seconds, the path to try it works, and someone is there to answer every comment.

## When to use

- A new product, major feature, free tool, public repo or report is going live.
- Deciding whether a launch platform (Product Hunt, deal sites, review directories) is worth the effort for your buyers.
- Planning the week around a launch date.

## Inputs

- Profile: `positioning`, `offer`, `channels.active`, `voice.personas`, `measurement`.
- What is launching, the date, the people available on launch day.
- Assets that exist: demo video or GIF, screenshots, FAQ, pricing, docs.

## Process

1. **Check the launch surface.** The first screen (homepage, product page or README) answers: what it is, who it is for, what it replaces or improves, what to do next. A visible demo shows value fast. Signup or install works end to end.
2. **Choose channels by where buyers are.** Founder and brand posts, email to existing contacts who opted in, relevant communities (within their rules), launch platforms only if their audience overlaps your ICP, review directories in your actual category. Deal platforms: check long-term support cost and commission before committing.
3. **Timeline:**
   ```
   T-7: positioning, screenshots, demo, FAQ final
   T-3: channel posts drafted, email ready, tracking links built
   T-1: test signup, install and demo paths; brief the team on replies
   Day 0: publish, respond to every comment, log feedback and known issues
   T+1: summarize results, fix obvious friction, follow up with engaged users
   ```
4. **Write per-channel posts** that sound like a person explaining what they built and why, not a press release. Rewrite for each channel.
5. **Response plan:** who answers comments on each channel, prepared answers for likely questions, where bugs and feedback get logged.
6. **Metrics:** one primary metric (for example qualified signups or activated users) and two supporting ones. Record visits, signups, activation and paid separately; platform rank or upvotes are not the goal.
7. **Next-day review:** what worked, what broke, which users to follow up with, what to change on the first screen.

## Output

```markdown
## Launch surface check (pass or fix, per item)
## Channel plan (channel, audience overlap, post draft, owner, time)
## Timeline with owners
## Response plan and FAQ
## Metrics and tracking links
## T+1 review template
```

## Gates

- No bought upvotes, reviews or fake accounts; reviews come from real users and follow each platform's rules on incentives ([evidence-and-claims](../b2b-growth-kit/policies/evidence-and-claims.md)).
- Publishing, emails and spend follow [approval-gates](../b2b-growth-kit/policies/approval-gates.md); emails go only to people who opted in or meet [outreach-compliance](../b2b-growth-kit/policies/outreach-compliance.md).
