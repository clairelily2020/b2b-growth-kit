# Link acquisition

Links still matter for search and for being cited by AI assistants. The durable ones are earned: they exist because a real audience finds the linked page useful. This skill favours those and treats paid placements as a flagged risk.

## When to use

- Monthly link plan, or after launching a page that deserves links.
- A site's authority is flat and content alone is not moving rankings.
- Someone proposes buying links or a sponsored post.

## Inputs

- Profile: `positioning`, `icp`, `competitors`, `offer`.
- Pages worth linking to (data, tools, templates, guides) and target pages to support.
- Optional: backlink exports for your site and competitors; citation lists from [geo-citation-scout](../geo-citation-scout/SKILL.md).

## Process

1. **Start from linkable assets.** Original data, benchmarks, free tools or templates, definitive guides, useful visuals. If you have none, the plan starts with creating one.
2. **Build the prospect list from evidence:** sites already cited for your topics by AI engines, pages linking to two or more competitors, industry associations, partner and integration directories, podcasts and newsletters in the ICP's world, review platforms.
3. **Vet each domain.** Real audience and organic traffic; topical relevance to the ICP; editorial standards (named authors, no "write for us, any topic" pages); fewer than about 10 outbound links per article; no sudden topic changes in the archive; not part of a private blog network. Read three recent articles before deciding.
4. **Choose the method per prospect:** data or story pitch (digital PR), guest article with real expertise, partner or integration listing, directory or review profile, community or podcast contribution.
5. **Plan anchors naturally:** mostly brand and URL anchors, a minority of descriptive anchors, no exact-match anchor repeated.
6. **Paid placements:** if anyone proposes one, state the search-guideline risk plainly. Any paid or sponsored link must be marked `rel="sponsored"` and treated as advertising, not as an SEO tactic. Spending follows the approval gate.
7. **Keep a registry:** domain, method, contact role, status, live URL, anchor, target page, date live, indexed date, cost if any.

## Output

```markdown
## Linkable assets (existing, and to create)
## Prospects
| Domain | Why relevant | Quality check result | Method | Target page | Priority |
## Pitch angles (one per prospect type)
## Registry updates
## Risks and blockers (for example: no real customers willing to leave reviews)
```

## Gates

- No private blog networks, link schemes, link exchanges at scale, or hidden paid links.
- Outreach to editors and site owners follows [outreach-compliance](../b2b-growth-kit/policies/outreach-compliance.md) and needs approval as a send.
- Any spend follows [approval-gates](../b2b-growth-kit/policies/approval-gates.md); domain metrics name their tool and date.
