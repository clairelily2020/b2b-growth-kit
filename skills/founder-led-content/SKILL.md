# Founder-led content

In early B2B, buyers trust a person before they trust a brand. Founder posts work when they share something specific the founder saw, decided or learned, in their own voice. They fail when they read like the company blog with a face attached.

## When to use

- Writing posts for the founder's personal LinkedIn or X.
- Turning a customer conversation, decision or mistake into a post.
- Planning a weekly founder content rhythm.
- For the company page, use [brand-social-content](../brand-social-content/SKILL.md).

## Inputs

- Profile: `voice` (tone, avoid list, the founder persona and topics), `positioning`, `claims`.
- Raw material from this week: product decisions, customer quotes (with permission or anonymized), market observations, numbers you can share, competitor moves seen in [competitive-intel](../competitive-intel/SKILL.md).

## Process

1. **Source topics from real events.** Good sources: a decision and why, a customer's exact problem, a surprising number from your own data, a mistake and what changed, a contrarian view you can back up. If nothing happened, do not invent it.
2. **One post, one job:** explain a pain, show evidence, make a point, or ask a concrete question.
3. **Write the hook** as the first line someone sees before "see more". Specific beats clever: name the role, situation, number or event.
4. **Body:** short paragraphs, plain language, one concrete detail per point. A personal opinion is allowed and labelled as such. No hashtag clusters, no emoji unless the founder uses them.
5. **CTA:** at most one, and light: a question, an offer to share a resource, or nothing. Links in posts can reduce reach on some platforms; place them where the founder prefers and say so.
6. **Platform fit:** LinkedIn tolerates 100-250 words and line breaks; X wants one sharp idea per post, or a thread of 5-12 posts for a full story.
7. **Cadence:** a sustainable rhythm (for example 2-3 LinkedIn posts and a few X posts a week) beats bursts. Plan with [content-calendar](../content-calendar/SKILL.md).
8. **After publishing:** reply to comments within the first hours; that is where relationships start.

## Output

```markdown
## Post drafts
Platform / job of the post / source material used
Hook
Body
CTA (if any)
Proof or permission note (for any number, quote or customer reference)
## Optional: 2 alternative hooks
```

## Gates

- Numbers, quotes and customer stories are real and permitted; see [evidence-and-claims](../b2b-growth-kit/policies/evidence-and-claims.md).
- The founder reviews every post; drafts are not posted on their behalf without approval ([approval-gates](../b2b-growth-kit/policies/approval-gates.md)).
- Nothing from `claims.banned` or `voice.avoid`.
