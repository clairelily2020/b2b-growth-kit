# Brand social content

The company account carries facts: what the product does, what the data shows, what customers achieved. The founder carries opinions. Keeping that split makes both more credible.

## When to use

- Weekly company page or brand account content.
- Supporting a launch, an event, or an ad campaign with organic posts.
- Repurposing a report, case or product update into social formats.
- For the founder's personal account, use [founder-led-content](../founder-led-content/SKILL.md).

## Inputs

- Profile: `voice.personas` (brand persona), `positioning`, `claims`, `channels`, `offer.utm`.
- Material: product updates and screenshots, data from your own product or research, customer quotes with permission, competitive or market observations.
- Channel: `linkedin` or `x`.

## Process

1. **Weekly mix** (adjust to capacity): one product or how-to post, one insight or data post, one customer or use-case post. On X, add short daily posts and one weekly thread that tells one story fully.
2. **Pick the format by job:**
   | Format | Best for |
   |---|---|
   | Carousel or document | Step-by-step how-tos, data visuals, full case studies |
   | Short video (under 60 s, captioned) | Product action, customer testimony |
   | Single image | One clear chart or screenshot with one point |
   | Text | Opinions and quick takes; good for testing topics |
   | Thread (X) | A case or insight told in 5-12 posts, hook first |
3. **Write:** the hook in the first line; one point per post; facts with sources; customer quotes verbatim and permitted. Link with UTMs from `offer.utm` when a link is used.
4. **Visual brief** for each post: what the image or slides show, using real product UI or real data.
5. **After publishing:** reply to comments, and engage with a few relevant accounts' recent posts with genuine comments. No engagement pods or reciprocal-like schemes.
6. **Log metrics** after about 24-48 hours: impressions, engagement rate, clicks, follows. Compare formats over weeks, not single posts.

## Output

```markdown
Channel / week
| Day | Topic | Format | Hook | Body | Visual brief | Link and UTM | Status |
## Thread draft (X, if planned)
## Metrics from last period and what to change
```
