# Workflow: product launch

**Goal:** a launch that brings the right users to a working first experience, and a list of what to fix the next day.
**Stage:** any release worth announcing.
**Time box:** T-7 to T+1.

| Step | Skill | Input | Hand-off artifact |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [campaign-brief](../../campaign-brief/SKILL.md) | The release | Brief with one KPI and test window |
| 2 | [launch-campaign](../../launch-campaign/SKILL.md) | Brief, assets | Launch surface check, channel plan, timeline |
| 3 | [landing-page](../../landing-page/SKILL.md) or first-screen copy | Positioning | Page or README first screen |
| 4 | [founder-led-content](../../founder-led-content/SKILL.md) + [brand-social-content](../../brand-social-content/SKILL.md) | Channel plan | Launch posts per channel |
| 5 | [ad-creative](../../ad-creative/SKILL.md) (optional) | Angles | Demo clip or visuals |
| 6 | [campaign-review](../../campaign-review/SKILL.md) | All assets | Ship call and preflight |
| 7 | Approvals, then launch day | | Published URLs, response log |
| 8 | [marketing-reporting](../../marketing-reporting/SKILL.md) | T+1 data | Launch report and fix list |

**Gates:** no bought votes or reviews ([evidence-and-claims](../policies/evidence-and-claims.md)); publishing and emails per [approval-gates](../policies/approval-gates.md) and [outreach-compliance](../policies/outreach-compliance.md).

**Done when:** every comment is answered, the T+1 review is written, and the top friction points are assigned.
