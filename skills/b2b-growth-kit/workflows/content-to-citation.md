# Workflow: content to citation

**Goal:** pages that win specific buyer questions in search and in AI assistants, with measured before-and-after citations.
**Stage:** first customers onward.
**Time box:** audit in week 1, pages over weeks 2-6, re-check after 4-8 weeks.

| Step | Skill | Input | Hand-off artifact |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [geo-citation-scout](../../geo-citation-scout/SKILL.md) | Product and buyer questions | Citation report with weak and missing positions |
| 2 | [seo](../../seo/SKILL.md) | Same questions, search data | Keyword clusters and page plan |
| 3 | [answer-engine-content](../../answer-engine-content/SKILL.md) | Priority gaps | Claim table, outline, draft |
| 4 | [article-writer](../../article-writer/SKILL.md) (for long-form) | Step 3 | House-style article |
| 5 | Fact check from [answer-engine-content](../../answer-engine-content/references/fact-check-gates.md) | Steps 3-4 | Seven-gate report, no critical or high findings |
| 6 | [cms-draft-patcher](../../cms-draft-patcher/SKILL.md) | Approved draft | CMS draft, verification checklist |
| 7 | [prepublish-qa](../../prepublish-qa/SKILL.md) | Preview URL | Findings and verdict |
| 8 | Approval by `approvals.publish`, then publishing | | Live URL |
| 9 | [link-acquisition](../../link-acquisition/SKILL.md) + [reddit-community](../../reddit-community/SKILL.md) | Community-led and authority gaps | Earned link and community plan |
| 10 | [geo-citation-scout](../../geo-citation-scout/SKILL.md) re-run | After 4-8 weeks | Diff of citations against the first report |

**Gates:** [evidence-and-claims](../policies/evidence-and-claims.md) on every page; publishing per [approval-gates](../policies/approval-gates.md).

**Done when:** pages are live, the re-run shows which positions moved, and the next set of questions is chosen from the diff.
