# Workflow: account-based outbound

**Goal:** booked first meetings with qualified accounts from a small, researched list.
**Stage:** first customers to repeatability.
**Time box:** list in week 1, sequences over weeks 2-4.

| Step | Skill | Input | Hand-off artifact |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [prospect-list-builder](../../prospect-list-builder/SKILL.md) | ICP, sources | Tiered account and contact CSV with research angles and suppression status |
| 2 | [social-engagement](../../social-engagement/SKILL.md) (optional) | Tier A contacts | Engagement log, warm signals |
| 3 | [cold-email](../../cold-email/SKILL.md) | Tier A and B rows with angles | Sequence or one-to-one drafts, skipped rows |
| 4 | [linkedin-outbound](../../linkedin-outbound/SKILL.md) | Same list, owner per prospect | LinkedIn steps and pacing plan |
| 5 | [send-preflight](../../send-preflight/SKILL.md) | Steps 3-4, sending setup | Gate table: ready for decision or blocked |
| 6 | Approval by `approvals.send`, then sending in your tools | | Send log |
| 7 | [revenue-handoff](../../revenue-handoff/SKILL.md) | Replies | Qualification, handoff notes, call guides |
| 8 | [performance-diagnosis](../../performance-diagnosis/SKILL.md) | Results after the window | What to change in list, copy or channel |

**Gates:** [outreach-compliance](../policies/outreach-compliance.md) for every touch; one owner per prospect; sends only after step 5 passes and step 6 is given.

**Done when:** the batch has run its full sequence, every reply is handled, suppression is updated, and the next batch's changes are written down.
