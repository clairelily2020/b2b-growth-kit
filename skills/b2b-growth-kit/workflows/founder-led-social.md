# Workflow: founder-led social

**Goal:** a steady, credible presence for the founder and the company that produces conversations with the ICP.
**Stage:** any; most valuable before and during first customers.
**Time box:** plan monthly, run weekly.

| Step | Skill | Input | Hand-off artifact |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [content-calendar](../../content-calendar/SKILL.md) | Monthly theme, anchor dates, capacity | Two-to-four-week calendar |
| 2 | [founder-led-content](../../founder-led-content/SKILL.md) | Week's real material | Founder post drafts |
| 3 | [brand-social-content](../../brand-social-content/SKILL.md) | Product updates, data, cases | Company post drafts and visual briefs |
| 4 | [social-engagement](../../social-engagement/SKILL.md) | Target list | Daily comments and replies |
| 5 | [creator-partnerships](../../creator-partnerships/SKILL.md) (optional) | Budget, ICP | Creator shortlist and outreach |
| 6 | Approval per post by the account owner, then publishing | | Post URLs |
| 7 | [marketing-reporting](../../marketing-reporting/SKILL.md) | Post metrics, conversations started | Weekly social section, what to change |

**Gates:** real material only ([evidence-and-claims](../policies/evidence-and-claims.md)); each post approved by the person or role who owns the account ([approval-gates](../policies/approval-gates.md)).

**Done when:** the month's calendar ran, metrics are logged, and conversations that showed interest are handed to [revenue-handoff](../../revenue-handoff/SKILL.md).
