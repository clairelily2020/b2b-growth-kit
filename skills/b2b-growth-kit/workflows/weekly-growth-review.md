# Workflow: weekly growth review

**Goal:** a 30-60 minute weekly loop that turns data into the next week's priorities.
**Stage:** any.
**Time box:** weekly; deeper retro every two weeks.

| Step | Skill | Input | Hand-off artifact |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [ops-brief](../../ops-brief/SKILL.md) | Queues, approvals, logs | Blockers and the 3-6 unblock actions |
| 2 | [marketing-reporting](../../marketing-reporting/SKILL.md) | Channel and CRM data | Weekly report with unknowns labelled |
| 3 | [performance-diagnosis](../../performance-diagnosis/SKILL.md) | The one metric that moved most unexpectedly | Hypotheses and next test |
| 4 | [market-signal-brief](../../market-signal-brief/SKILL.md) (if something new appeared) | New observations | Experiment brief or a decision not to act |
| 5 | [growth-plan](../../growth-plan/SKILL.md) | Steps 1-4 | Next week's priorities (max 5); retro every second week |

**Gates:** definitions from [metrics-definitions](../policies/metrics-definitions.md); no actions executed from the review without [approval-gates](../policies/approval-gates.md).

**Done when:** next week has at most five priorities with owners, and every open experiment has a status and a check date.
