# Workflow: positioning sprint

**Goal:** a positioning, offer ladder and message house backed by real buyer evidence, ready to brief every channel.
**Stage:** validation or first customers; also after a pivot or when copy feels generic.
**Time box:** about two weeks.

| Step | Skill | Input | Hand-off artifact |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [voice-of-customer](../../voice-of-customer/SKILL.md) | 5-15 interviews across buyers, churned users and non-buyers | Interview synthesis with verbatim quotes |
| 2 | [competitive-intel](../../competitive-intel/SKILL.md) | Named competitors and current alternatives | Dated fact registry, alternatives map |
| 3 | [icp-and-positioning](../../icp-and-positioning/SKILL.md) | Steps 1-2 | ICP table, positioning card, one-liner, message house |
| 4 | [offer-design](../../offer-design/SKILL.md) | Step 3, delivery capacity | Offer ladder and channel-to-offer map |
| 5 | [landing-page](../../landing-page/SKILL.md) | Steps 3-4 | First-screen copy for the main offer |
| 6 | [campaign-review](../../campaign-review/SKILL.md) | Steps 3-5 | Findings and ship call |

**Gates:** every differentiator traces to evidence ([evidence-and-claims](../policies/evidence-and-claims.md)); publishing the page follows [approval-gates](../policies/approval-gates.md).

**Done when:** the profile's `icp`, `positioning` and `offer` sections are updated with the accepted versions, and the evidence gaps are listed with a plan to close them.
