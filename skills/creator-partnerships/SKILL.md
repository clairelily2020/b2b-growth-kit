# Creator partnerships

In B2B, a niche expert with a few thousand of the right followers can outperform a large account. The work is finding real audience fit, proposing something useful for the creator's audience, and measuring it honestly.

## When to use

- Starting or expanding a creator or expert program.
- Evaluating a sponsorship offer from a newsletter, podcast or creator.
- Renewing or extending an existing partnership.

## Inputs

- Profile: `icp`, `positioning`, `offer`, `claims`, `offer.utm`, `approvals.spend`.
- Budget range and what you can offer besides money (product access, data, co-created research).
- Optional: creators who covered competitors or adjacent topics.

## Process

1. **Search where the ICP learns:** topic searches on LinkedIn and X, YouTube how-tos and reviews in your category, niche newsletters and podcasts, industry association speakers, and who your best customers follow.
2. **Score** (out of 100): audience fit 40 (their audience matches the ICP's roles and industries), engagement quality 30 (real comments from practitioners, not just likes), topic relevance 20 (they discuss your problem space), originality 10. Record follower counts but do not rank by them.
3. **Check for red flags:** sudden follower spikes, engagement from unrelated accounts, many undisclosed sponsored posts, reputational issues.
4. **Choose a format:** sponsored post or newsletter slot (clearly disclosed), product access in exchange for an honest review (no editorial control), co-created content or research, joint webinar or live session.
5. **First message:** reference a specific piece they made, explain why their audience would care, propose one concrete format, state what you offer. Each message is individual.
6. **Agree terms in writing:** deliverables, dates, disclosure, usage rights, payment, what happens if the content underperforms. Never script fake enthusiasm or require a positive review.
7. **Attribution:** a dedicated UTM link or code per creator; measure qualified signups and pipeline, not views alone.
8. **Track:** creator, platform, score, status (identified, contacted, negotiating, live, completed), content URL, cost, attributed results.
