# Discovery and demo call guide

A 15-20 minute call. The goal is to learn whether there is a real problem worth solving now, and to agree on a concrete next step. A good call can end with "not a fit".

## Before the call

- Two minutes of account research: what they do, recent news or triggers, the context of their reply.
- Prepare one example that uses their real situation (their market, their event, their use case), built from public or first-party data you are allowed to use.
- Confirm the meeting a day before with the agenda in one line.

## Structure

**Open (2 min).** Thank them, state the plan: "I'll ask how you handle this today, show how it would work for your case, and you tell me honestly whether it solves something." Confirm the time.

**Discovery (3-5 min).** Ask about the last real instance:
- How are you handling this today, and who does it?
- What happens when it goes wrong or takes too long?
- What have you tried before?
- When is the next moment this matters (event, quarter, launch)?
- Who else would weigh in on a decision?

Listen more than you talk. Write down their exact words.

**Show (5-7 min).** Show only what maps to what they said, on their example, live if possible. Skip features they did not ask about.

**Confirm value (2 min).**
- How does this compare with what you do now?
- What would it change for your team if it worked?

**Next step (3 min).** Propose one concrete step that matches their intent: a reviewed sample, a pilot with a scope and success criteria, a call with the other decision maker, or pricing for their case. Agree on a date. Close with "If it's not a fit, just tell me; that's useful too."

## After the call

- Send a short recap the same day: their problem in their words, what you showed, the agreed next step and date.
- Update the handoff note and propose the lifecycle change with the evidence.
- If they said no, record why and when it might be worth revisiting.

## Signals worth noting

| Signal | Usually means |
|---|---|
| Asks about price unprompted | Intent is real; confirm budget owner and timing |
| Asks to bring a colleague | Champion forming; plan the second call around the colleague's concerns |
| "Send me something" without a date | Polite exit; ask what specifically would be useful and when |
| Compares with a named alternative | Evaluation underway; ask what matters most in the comparison |
