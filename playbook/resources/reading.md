# Reading, by current problem

A short list tied to the work in this playbook. Each link is a starting point; record one thing after reading: which decision it changes, and what local evidence would support the change.

| Current problem | Read | How to use it |
|---|---|---|
| How a first marketing lead should organize the work | [MKT1 Method](https://newsletter.mkt1.co/p/mkt1-method) | Compare with your team's skills and distribution options and adjust this week's work; do not copy another company's org chart |
| How to judge product-market fit | [First Round: Levels of PMF](https://www.firstround.com/levels) | Check evidence of demand, satisfaction and delivery efficiency rather than a fixed revenue threshold |
| How customers understand the product and its alternatives | [April Dunford on positioning](https://www.aprildunford.com/category/positioning) | Re-interview customers about what they really compared you with |
| Using AI-generated content for search | [Google Search Central on generative AI content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content) | Check value, accuracy and how the content will be maintained |
| Whether a conversion action is used for bidding | [Google Ads: primary and secondary conversion actions](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/11461796?hl=en) | Check actions, campaign goals and the custom-goal exception |
| A broader go-to-market reading list | [B2B Playbook by Ivan Xu](https://github.com/weilun88313/B2B-Playbook) | Choose further reading there; its ratings are not an independent performance audit |
