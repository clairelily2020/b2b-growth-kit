# Pre-launch checklist

The minimum set for S0 to S1. Pick what applies to the deal you are selling; not everything has to be done at once.

## First, be able to explain and deliver

- [ ] One buying job, its user, its payer, the trigger and the cases you will not serve.
- [ ] The customer's current alternative, including manual work, outsourcing and doing nothing.
- [ ] A sample they can check; real, simulated and unverified data are labelled.
- [ ] What the product does and does not do yet; sales and the website use the same version.
- [ ] A quote with scope, allowance, period, support, overage and failure handling.
- [ ] A delivery owner, a support channel, and a process for errors and refunds.

## Then, be findable and trustworthy

- [ ] Domain, work email, team identity, product screens and contact details are consistent.
- [ ] The homepage says who it is for and which job it does; the main CTA leads to a real next step.
- [ ] The demo or trial path works, mobile forms work, and submissions get a clear confirmation.
- [ ] Privacy notice, terms and the selling entity reflect the real business; legal questions go to a qualified person.
- [ ] Testimonials, customer logos, scale claims and results have evidence and permission.
- [ ] Someone handles unsubscribes, feedback and complaints.

## Before spending on ads

- [ ] Ads, content, page and sample promise the same thing.
- [ ] Source, signup, booking, meeting held and payment are defined separately.
- [ ] Test and internal traffic is marked; success events are not button clicks.
- [ ] The CRM has at least company, job, owner, source and next-step date.
- [ ] Experiment window, budget cap, stop rule and review date are written down.
- [ ] Someone specific follows up leads and collects payment, not just "marketing".

## Can wait

A full brand book, complex scoring models, large numbers of SEO pages, a full customer data platform, multi-layer automation, accounts on every social network. Do not buy for work you do not have yet. If you sell to large enterprises, security and procurement material may need to come earlier, not later.
