# A 90-day plan you can trim

A working suggestion, not a fixed budget or set of acceptance gates. Products that already have customers can skip some of the setup but should still fill missing evidence.

| Weeks | Question this round answers | Suggested deliverables | Decision at the end |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | Who needs us, and at what moment | Interview notes, buying-job cards, alternatives, counter-evidence | Narrow or change the segment |
| 3-4 | Will people try it and pay | Sample, pilot quote, a basic page, real conversations | Fix the offer or delivery before adding channels |
| 5-6 | Where does first value stall | Customer worksheets, task demos, event checks | Fix experience, data or follow-up |
| 7-8 | Which path is worth repeating | One complete experiment in targeted outbound, search or a partnership | Continue, change one condition, or stop |
| 9-10 | Can we produce credible proof | Permitted feedback, draft case study, distribution plan | Extend to similar audiences |
| 11-12 | Is more investment worth it | Cohort results, costs, sales capacity, next-stage plan | Scale what is proven or go back a stage |

Every week, keep one customer conversation, one delivery check, one data check and one decision review. Schedule around the people you have; do not let content production fill every hour while nobody follows up leads.

Several channels can be evaluated in parallel, item by item; you do not need to wait for one directory or outlet to prove itself before researching others. Budgets follow your current approvals, not the examples in this playbook.

Close each week with the [review template](../templates/weekly-review.md), and keep unproven conclusions labelled as hypotheses.
