# A two-person team with AI: faster preparation, clearer responsibility

Written for a founder plus one marketer. The split below is a suggestion, not a job description, and it grants no one permission for external actions.

| Work | Founder | Marketer | AI can take |
|---|---|---|---|
| Segments, offer, budget | Decides and approves | Collects customer feedback | Organizing evidence, comparing options |
| Content and partnerships | Owns promises and negotiations | Scheduling, assets, partner material | First drafts, rewrites, asset checks |
| Demos and follow-up | Qualifies needs, quotes | Meeting prep, notes, next-step tracking | Account research, note drafts |
| Data and reviews | Decides continue, change or stop | Checks delivery and owners | Deduplication, definition checks, anomaly summaries |

Sending, publishing, buying, ad changes and contact uploads happen under specific approvals; research results never trigger them automatically. That does not mean asking the founder at every step: work inside an agreed scope should flow without interruption. The rules are in [approval-gates](../../skills/b2b-growth-kit/policies/approval-gates.md).

## A reusable loop

Customer interview, then extract quotes and unknowns, update the buying job, write a content brief, verify claims by hand, adapt for each channel, publish with approval, save the link, and watch later usage and sales outcomes.

AI can draft competitive analysis, but prices, features and product claims are checked on the original pages. It can summarize interviews, not invent buying motives the customer never stated. It can produce animation, not fake product operations or customer proof.

## Before automating

Run it by hand first, with clear inputs, outputs, failure conditions and an owner. Only work that repeats and has a stable definition is worth automating. "Configured", "scheduled job ran", "report saved", "notification delivered" and "business outcome achieved" are five different states.

The weekly summary for the founder keeps only: what happened, how far the evidence goes, the current problem, and the recommended action. Raw reports stay in the knowledge base; nobody is expected to read them all.

Use the documents, CRM and analytics you already have before adding tools. Before adding an agent or connector, say what work it removes and what maintenance it adds. Number of tools is not team capacity.
