# S3: Keep buyers getting value

**When:** acquisition shows signs of life, but people stop after signup, never finish the first task, or renew for unclear reasons.

First define "the first time it was useful". For a data product, logging in is not value, and an export does not prove a sales result. A signal closer to value might be the customer finishing a reviewed list they can use in their own workflow. The definition comes from the customer's job and what the product can actually do.

## A worksheet per customer

Record the buying job, the first-value goal, the expected date, the owner, evidence of completion, blockers, and the next time they will need the product. Separate experience problems: cannot find the feature, data does not fit, credits or billing confuse them, or their work has not started yet.

Self-serve customers get task-based guidance and help content; complex customers get one real working session. Do not force everyone through the same expensive onboarding.

## Renewal and expansion

- After the first delivery, confirm the result was usable; keep their words and the context.
- Before the next occurrence of the job, send a reminder or sample relevant to it; the frequency follows the real usage cycle.
- Several events or several teams may justify a bundle. Do not sell an annual plan before a second job has appeared.
- Handle trust issues (credits charged on failed tasks, data freshness) and record the fix. A refund or credit return does not by itself explain a later renewal.
- Customers who succeeded and are willing can be asked for a review or a referral. Never make a review a condition of service or compensation.

## Which numbers to watch

By first-payment month and buying job: first-value completion rate, time to value, repeat use, renewal and churn reasons. With a small subscriber base, listing account-level changes is often more honest than a volatile percentage.

GRR = (starting recurring revenue - churn - contraction) / starting recurring revenue. NRR adds expansion from the same cohort. Use one currency and revenue period, exclude new customers, and keep one-off packages out of subscription retention.

A renewal is evidence of customer value; do not count it as new acquisition from the latest channel. Collect [proof cards](../templates/proof-card.md) before wider brand distribution.
