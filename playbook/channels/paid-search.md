# Google search ads

**Fits:** S1-S4; clear search demand and a promise you can deliver.

**Before you start:** confirm the search intent, the matching page, the success event (payment or acquisition) and who follows up. Without measurement, run only a controlled exploration and do not claim efficient acquisition.

**How to run it:** group by buying job. Check actual search terms against your matches; the ad and the page make the same promise. Track brand, competitor and job terms separately, so returning brand searchers are not counted as all-new demand.

**Materials:** keyword and negative sheet, intent pages, samples, event checks, budget and stop rules. Review negatives term by term; do not permanently block a whole word family without checking.

**How to judge:** first query relevance and real business actions, then qualified accounts, opportunities and payments. Platform CTR or "conversions" are not business ROI.

**Adjust or stop:** fix wrong pages, expired event terms or polluted measurement first. Scaling looks at mature cohorts and follow-up cost, not a fixed conversion-count threshold.

**Field note:** two pages for two sides of a market (for example sellers and buyers at the same event) are different demand paths, not an A/B test of one audience.
