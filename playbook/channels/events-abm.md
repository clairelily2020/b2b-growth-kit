# Events, small gatherings and ABM

**Fits:** S1-S4; target accounts can be listed and each is worth manual work.

**Before you start:** prepare account priorities, a reason to meet before the event, who takes meetings and who follows up after. Attending is not a buying signal and a badge scan is not an opportunity.

**How to run it:** research the job and roles for a small set of target accounts and create useful pre-event material. Consider a roundtable, workshop or dinner only if the right people will come. Compare a booth, a joint event and plain attendance on total cost and meeting opportunities; do not assume a big booth works best.

**Materials:** account plan, pre-booked meetings, on-site questions, a handoff sheet, follow-up content, and travel and staff costs.

**How to judge:** completed meaningful conversations, agreed next steps, opportunity progress and revenue once mature. Keep existing and new opportunities apart.

**Adjust or stop:** scans, business cards or event exposure alone are not success. If you cannot book target people before the event, rethink the investment rather than hoping for random floor traffic.

**Field note:** event platforms are useful for finding or hosting gatherings; whether a given event reaches your ICP is checked event by event.
