# Creators, newsletters and podcasts

**Fits:** S1-S4; you have a credible sample and want to reach a professional audience where it reads and listens.

**Before you start:** confirm the audience's roles, regions, real engagement and the collaboration format. A large following is not a large buyer base.

**How to run it:** use one brief to check audience and deliverables with each candidate: topic, format, date, how long it stays up, content review, fee, reuse rights and data shared back. Give each placement its own trackable source and matching page. After it goes live, check the real link and wording, then follow downstream results.

**Materials:** the [partner brief](../templates/partner-brief.md), product facts, a sample you can show, assets, source links and an acceptance checklist.

**How to judge:** first accept the content and links, then look at visits, signups, qualified accounts, usage and payments. Set the window by the sales cycle, not the day of publication.

**Adjust or stop:** do not book more when there is only a reach promise, the audience cannot be confirmed, or delivery evidence keeps missing. Costs include platform fees and production; a quote is not a payment.

**Field note:** a placement that produces attributable signups is a stronger signal than a platform's own recommendation; neither validates your ICP on its own.
