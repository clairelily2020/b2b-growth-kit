# SEO, event pages and AI search visibility

**Fits:** S1-S4; the product answers long-lived search questions and you can keep information current.

**Before you start:** choose queries tied to the customer's job; verify data sources, dates and product promises.

**How to run it:** build a few genuinely useful task pages, comparison pages and event pages; check indexing and search performance. Event pages state the edition, dates, coverage and update status; comparison pages give checkable differences. Turn customers' real follow-up questions into supported answers and distribute them where they fit.

**Materials:** topic and query sheet, source records, samples, update dates, internal links, Search Console and business events.

**How to judge:** relevant queries, qualified visits to target pages and later usage. A brand mention in an AI answer, AI referral visits and revenue are different evidence.

**Adjust or stop:** if bulk pages lack standalone value or you cannot maintain them, stop adding pages. Never promise citations in ChatGPT or Perplexity, and do not treat one answer to one prompt as a stable ranking.

**Field note:** an old estimate of a competitor's traffic share is a point-in-time guess, not their channel's real contribution. Google warns that mass-produced content without added value can fall under its spam policies.

Source: [Google's guidance on generative AI content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content).
