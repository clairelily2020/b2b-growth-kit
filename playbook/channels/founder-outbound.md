# Founder-led sales and targeted outbound

**Fits:** S0-S2; a clear set of buyers worth researching by hand, and someone to take the conversations.

**Before you start:** the list comes from an explainable business fit and real triggers; you have a concrete sample and a scoped offer. Check the rules for each recipient's region and channel before the first touch; identify yourself clearly and give an easy way out.

**How to run it:** research the company and the buying job first, then find the relevant role. Explain why you are reaching out with one checkable observation and propose one low-effort next step. Log positive, negative, automatic and unsubscribe replies; an out-of-office is not interest. Move interested people into a needs conversation with a named owner and date.

**Materials:** account sheet, source evidence, a short message, a sample, reply categories and a do-not-contact list. Use the CRM you have; sequencing and data tools are candidates, not a bundle to buy at once.

**How to judge:** ICP-fit conversations, qualified opportunities and paid pilots. Send volume and open rates are diagnostics only. Change one main assumption in the list or message at a time.

**Adjust or stop:** if the list cannot explain fit, unsubscribe handling fails, or conversations keep showing no relevance, fix that first. Never compensate for the wrong segment with more volume.

**Field note:** automatic and negative replies are not interest; people who decline an interview leave the interview queue for good.
