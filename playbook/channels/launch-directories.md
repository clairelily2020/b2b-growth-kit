# Launch platforms, deal sites, reviews and directories

**Fits:** S1-S3; the product can be tried and the platform's audience overlaps your buyers.

**Before you start:** finish the usage path, support and product fact checks before launching. Ask first whether the platform reaches buyers, then decide whether a launch is worth the effort.

**How to run it:** on launch platforms, use a clear product demo and real engagement, and track user quality. On deal sites, calculate long-term usage and support cost first and limit unsustainable promises. On review directories, choose the actual product category; reviews come from real experience.

**Materials:** launch assets, a task demo, FAQ, a support rota, source records, review invitations and permissions.

**How to judge:** record rank, visits, signups, activation, payment and continued use separately. Deal-site revenue is net of commission, refunds and delivery cost.

**Adjust or stop:** reduce effort when there is buzz but no fitting users. Never buy reviews or manufacture independent endorsements; reviews from free or discounted users follow the platform's current rules.

**Field note:** launch experience from consumer products is a reusable checklist, but its growth results do not transfer to enterprise purchasing.
