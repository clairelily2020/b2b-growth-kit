## Which one first

Known accounts and a higher deal size: founder conversations and targeted outbound. Clear search demand: search pages and controlled ads. Buyers rely on peer trust: advisers, professional communities and niche content. You need organizer or channel reach: a scoped partner pilot.

Usually keep both demand capture and trust building, in proportions set by the current problem. There is no universal 70/20/10 split. Broad tech media, generic AI directories and big booths can stay in the candidate pool until the audience is shown to be worth it.

Every channel needs the same basics: one clear offer, one working entry point, one source record, one person to follow up and one review.
