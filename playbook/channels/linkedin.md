# LinkedIn: founder content, relationships and outreach

**Fits:** S0-S4; buyers have a professional identity and talk about work problems.

**Before you start:** the profile explains your role and the product; prepare one long-running theme, work samples and real opinions. Content reach and private messages are different actions.

**How to run it:** collect the problems buyers and peers keep discussing and contribute specific experience. Publish a judgment or process with its reasoning and answer real follow-ups. For clearly fitting accounts, research by hand and give context when you reach out; an accepted connection is not consent to a promotional sequence.

**Materials:** profile, theme list, samples, comment and reply log, CRM source fields.

**How to judge:** engagement from target roles, relevant profile visits, inbound questions and follow-up conversations. Followers and impressions do not prove pipeline.

**Adjust or stop:** if the content only attracts peers and tool enthusiasts, change topics and distribution. Do not treat a stranger's job change as strong buying intent.

**Field note:** two versions of the message are worth keeping: the budget owner cares about investment and decisions, the practitioner about the task and workload. Treat both as hypotheses to refine in conversation.
