# Consultants, associations and joint education

**Fits:** S1-S3; the product needs industry explanation and advisers or associations reach the buyers.

**Before you start:** confirm whom the partner serves, whether they agree with your method, and whether vendors are allowed to take part. A membership does not grant marketing rights.

**How to run it:** give the adviser a task sample they can try themselves and invite them to improve the method with you. Formats: tutorials, joint workshops, evaluation checklists or referrals. With associations, check eligibility, benefits and actual events before discussing sponsorship.

**Materials:** a teaching outline, a verifiable sample, partnership boundaries, an audience description, content and trademark permissions.

**How to judge:** target buyers attending, specific questions, sample usage and follow-up conversations; also record how adviser feedback improved the product.

**Adjust or stop:** skip badges, member plaques or events unrelated to target roles. An adviser willing to talk is not willing to recommend, and willing to recommend is not delivering customers.

**Field note:** a list of candidate advisers and "to contact" labels is a research pool, not a set of current partnerships.
