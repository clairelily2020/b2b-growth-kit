# Affiliates, referrals and resellers

**Fits:** S2-S4; a clear conversion path, known margin and partner support.

**Before you start:** define paid conversions, refunds, repeat customers, brand terms and commission ownership; check tracking and payouts. Connecting a platform is not building a channel.

**How to run it:** prioritize partners who already have relevant content or customer relationships; give them product facts, a demo, links and editorial angles. Track application, acceptance, published content, qualified referrals, payment and confirmed commission as separate stages. Keep an eye on real placements and dead links.

**Materials:** partner tiers, product material, commission terms, source IDs, payout records and a brand-wording check.

**How to judge:** active publishing partners, qualified visits, confirmed orders, commission net of refunds, and customer quality.

**Adjust or stop:** many accepted partners but no publishing means fixing activation; clicks without target buyers means fixing partner selection. Coupon sites that intercept existing demand are not new demand.

**Field note:** a count of publisher placements is not a count of paying customers. Do not buy a second partner platform just because it has a B2B label.
