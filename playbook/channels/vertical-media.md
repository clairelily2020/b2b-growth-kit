# Vertical media, industry directories and sponsorship

**Fits:** S1-S4; buyers use specific trade media or procurement directories.

**Before you start:** separate the outlet's readers: organizers, service providers and buying companies are different audiences, even inside one industry.

**How to run it:** ask for the current media kit and audience breakdown; pick articles, features or listings that can explain the job. Be clear whether you are buying publication, distribution, leads or event benefits. When it goes live, check the actual placement, link, UTM, product wording and duration.

**Materials:** itemized quote, page assets, samples, UTMs, delivery dates, data reporting and the sales owner.

**How to judge:** verifiable visits to your own pages and what those visitors do next. The outlet's total traffic or subscriber count is not your placement's exposure.

**Adjust or stop:** a logo or banner with vague audience, delivery and measurement is a brand budget decision, not predictable acquisition.

**Field note:** buying or creating a directory listing and getting customers from it are two separate acceptance steps. Old quotes are not current prices.
