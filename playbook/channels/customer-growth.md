# Customer education, nurture, referrals and expansion

**Fits:** S1-S4; you have trials or customers and a next job exists.

**Before you start:** group people by real lifecycle stage and usage need; set up unsubscribe and preference management.

**How to run it:** help those who have not completed the first task; with those who have, confirm the result and the next piece of work. Nurture content serves a specific evaluation question; not everyone gets the same long sequence. Ask satisfied, willing customers for referrals or reviews.

**Materials:** task tutorials, usage signals, customer questions, next-job dates, feedback and referral records.

**How to judge:** first value, repeat use, renewal and qualified referrals, calculated on the original customer cohort. Expansion from existing customers is not new acquisition.

**Adjust or stop:** if email volume rises but users have no task, complaints increase or messages repeat, lower the frequency and fix the grouping.

**Field note:** handling a billing complaint well is a service lesson worth keeping; the timing alone does not prove that a refund caused a later renewal.
