# Organizers, national pavilions and service partners

**Fits:** S1-S4; your results can be built into someone else's pre-event service.

**Before you start:** decide who the buyer is: the organizer, the pavilion organizer, a service provider or the exhibitor. Confirm separately whether the partner will take on promotion and delivery.

**How to run it:** pick one event with a limited scope and agree a pilot with the partner (an exhibitor toolkit, event selection research or a pre-event account service). Settle inputs and usage rights, who receives the output, support, fees, referrals and ownership of results before expanding to more events.

**Materials:** a one-page partner proposal, pilot scope, a delivery sample, fees or revenue share, a support owner and an acceptance plan.

**How to judge:** real trials, qualified referrals, partners adopting it again, delivery cost and cash collected; not the number of institutions signed.

**Adjust or stop:** long procurement cycles, no access to end users, or rework on every project mean checking whether the service model pays. Government or pavilion subsidy conditions must be confirmed with the program itself.

**Field note:** exhibitor toolkits, national pavilions, group organizers and export-promotion bodies are worth researching as paths; eligibility and prices stay unverified until checked. Never promise official partnerships or subsidies.
