# LinkedIn Ads, Reddit Ads, Meta and retargeting

**Fits:** S2-S4; the promise, creative and success event are clear.

**Before you start:** decide whether this is education, re-engagement or direct acquisition. Verify audience sources and the right to use them; owning contact data does not mean you may upload it to an ad platform.

**How to run it:** test one clear audience and offer in a single channel first. When comparing audiences, keep creative, page, region and optimization event the same. Retargeting needs a usable audience and appropriate consent; showing ads repeatedly to a tiny group is not new demand.

**Materials:** native specs per channel, creative versions, pages, events, audience source records and a separate experiment card per test.

**How to judge:** meaningful actions from the target audience and later business results. CPM and CPC explain cost; they do not pick the winner.

**Adjust or stop:** cheap clicks from irrelevant accounts, a shallow success event or an unsuitable audience source mean no extra budget. Check platform capabilities and policies in official documentation before acting.

**Field note:** organic Reddit participation and Reddit Ads are two different jobs, and an old ad test does not prove today's audience plan works.
