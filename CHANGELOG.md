# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-26

First release, consolidating earlier separate projects by Claire into one kit.

- 39 skills in eight areas, rewritten to one structure (When to use, Inputs, Process, Output, Gates, References), each reading company facts from a profile instead of hardcoding them.
- Shared policies written once: approval gates, evidence and claims, metrics definitions, outreach compliance.
- Eight workflows: positioning sprint, account-based outbound, event ABM, content to citation, paid search launch, founder-led social, product launch, weekly growth review.
- Company profile in TOML with a fictional DemoCo example.
- Playbook translated to English: five stages, eight practices, fifteen channel cards, templates and anonymized field notes.
- Harness 1.0: TOML profiles, a new `handoff` workflow, 33 tests.
- Repository checker with link, structure, privacy and local denylist checks; CI enabled.
